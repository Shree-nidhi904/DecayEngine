How it is built

  • One policy and one skin. The engine knows nothing about what an item is. Each app adds a decay rule and a look.
  • Ghosted needed no change to the engine. A script in the check suite proves it on every run. Add a policy of your own.
  • Everything stays in your browser. No account, no server, no network calls.
  • Tested. Over two thousand tests across the engine, the shared shell and both apps.
  • Built with Kiro. Specs, steering files, hooks and a read-only reviewing agent, with a log of every session.
