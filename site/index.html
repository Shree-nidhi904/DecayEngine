Every item has a life

A fact or a friendship starts out alive. Left alone, it fades. Tend it in time and it stays. Let it go and it dies, but most things can come back.

  1. AliveFresh and safe for now.
  2. WeakBelow 60. Worth tending soon.
  3. DeadBelow 10. Gone, with a reason why.
  4. ResurrectedBrought back with a right answer or a reply.
