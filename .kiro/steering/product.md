# Product

## What it is

Decay Engine is a reusable skeleton for building applications about things that fade, die, and can be brought back to life.

## Who it's for

- Developers who want to build decay-style apps without writing the lifecycle engine from scratch
- Page maintainers who need to track and revive dead links
- Learners who want a gamified spaced-repetition system

## Core promise

**An app is a policy plus a skin. The engine never needs to change for a new app.**

A policy defines how something decays and how it revives. A skin defines what it looks like and what it's called. That's it.

## Proof apps

- **Link Cemetery**: Dead links on a page become tombstones. Revived from the Internet Archive. Decay is caused by failed link checks.
- **Study Dungeon**: Facts you're forgetting become monsters. Decay is caused by time passing without review.
- **Expiry Reaper**: A minimal third app that validates the promise—zero engine changes needed.
